# Anatoly Zenchenko — coaching synthesis (for George)

Source: 135 transcribed voice memos + text, Apr–Aug 2026. Teacher: Anatoly Zenchenko
(Yoga23 / Y23 method — biomechanically precise, diaphragm + psoas + bandhas).

## The single thesis (repeated 40+ ways)
Stop stabilizing with the **back and glutes**. Move the foundation into the **legs**.
When the legs hold the structure, the back has no reason to tense — and the chronic
right-side psoas spasm can finally release.

## The mechanism
- **Diaphragm = conductor; legs = orchestra.** Diaphragm starts a *wave* on a half-breath
  (always in tone, never fully relaxed). Impulse travels: diaphragm → psoas (iliopsoas)
  → adductor + quadriceps → knee → foot.
- **Backbend from the legs, not the low back.** Pelvis must travel *with* the ribcage as
  one unit. Weld the pelvis to the legs (by tucking the tail / clenching glutes) and you
  force a hinge — usually **L5–S1**.
- **Nutation everywhere; never counter-nutation.** Make the pelvis a **square, not a
  trapezoid.** Wings of the pelvis forward and slightly inward.
- **Mula bandha = attention, not tension.** ("не tension, а attention.") The body's own
  deep torso↔leg link. Precision, not clenching. Grows into daily life (stairs).
- **Engage from the FOOT.** Not just thigh — big-toe-to-heel line, active shin, inner knee
  → engages adductor. Foot instability = can't carry pelvis forward, can't exit bakasana.
- **Adductor UP / quadriceps DOWN (50/50)** in backbends. Don't overload the quad; "inflate"
  (раздувать) the front of the thigh and release deep in the hip.
- **Litmus test:** glutes, low back, sacrum must stay SILENT.

## George's diagnosis (the right side)
- Chronic right iliopsoas spasm; long football/soccer history in that region.
- Had a hernia. Teacher's hypothesis: instead of shifting load into the right leg, George
  drove down with the diaphragm → excess pressure low-right → hernia.
- **Do NOT relieve the psoas by tensing the glute** — that's tension-on-tension. Release it
  with mula bandha + leg load + attention.

## The prescription (what to keep doing)
1. Load the RIGHT leg — right hip joint / sub-gluteal fold, stable knee, from the foot up.
2. Pull mula bandha precisely (gentle — attention, not tension); navel in; float lower ribs.
3. Let the diaphragm initiate on a half-breath; send the impulse into the legs.
4. Never tuck the tail / clench glutes. Keep nutation. Pelvis = square.
5. Backbends: carry the pelvis forward with the spine; adductor up, quad releases down.
6. Engage from the foot: big-toe→heel line, active shin, inner knee, adductor.
7. Keep back / sacrum / glutes silent — that's the check.
8. Triangles (trikonasana) daily — the master drill for legs controlling the pelvis.
9. Patience: understanding ≠ doing; nerves need reps. "Vacuum-cleaner" refinement in circles.
